What is Quantum Chess?

Quantum Chess incorporates the principles of quantum mechanics into the traditional game of chess, creating a unique and challenging experience that will expand your understanding of quantum phenomena while testing your strategic thinking.

Features

Quantum Superposition

Pieces can exist in multiple states simultaneously, introducing probability and uncertainty to your strategic planning.

Quantum Entanglement

Link the fate of two pieces together, creating powerful combinations and strategic opportunities.

Quantum Measurement

Force an observation of the quantum state, collapsing superpositions into definite positions.

How to Play

Quantum Chess follows the familiar rules of traditional chess but adds quantum moves that allow pieces to enter superposition or become entangled with other pieces. Each turn, you can choose between a classical move or a quantum move, creating a rich strategic landscape that rewards creative thinking and adaptability.

The game includes an interactive tutorial that will guide you through the quantum mechanics concepts as they apply to chess, making it accessible for players of all backgrounds.
